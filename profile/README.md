Tributary Labs
==============

> Streaming payment channels on Stellar — deposit once, stream at a defined rate, claim anytime.

A payer deposits XLM and streams it to a recipient at a defined rate. The recipient claims whatever has streamed so far, at any moment.

### Focus

- Stream at a rate: Open a channel with a custom rate and deposit — 1 XLM per hour, or any schedule you set.
- Claim whenever: The recipient withdraws accrued funds at any time instead of waiting for the stream to end.
- Top up, don't restart: Adding to an existing channel keeps the schedule intact.
